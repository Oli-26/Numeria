## NEW MATH TOPICS
- category-theory: A framework to study mathematical structures and their relationships.
- algebraic-geometry: The study of geometric properties of solutions to polynomial equations.
- optimization: Techniques for finding the best solution in a given scenario.
- game-theory: Mathematical models of strategic interaction between rational decision-makers.
- numerical-analysis: Methods for approximating mathematical problems using numerical techniques.
- mathematical-logic: The study of formal logic within mathematics.
- lie-theory: The study of continuous symmetries in mathematics.
- representation-theory: How algebraic structures can be represented by linear transformations.
- knot-theory: The study of mathematical knots and their properties.
- ergodic-theory: The study of dynamical systems with an invariant measure.
- harmonic-analysis: The study of the representation of functions or signals as the superposition of basic waves.
- mathematical-physics: The development of mathematical methods for solving problems in physics.
- dynamical-systems: The study of systems that evolve over time according to a set of rules.
- p-adic-analysis: A branch of number theory that studies p-adic numbers and their applications.
- model-theory: The study of formal languages and their interpretations.
- type-theory: A branch of mathematical logic that classifies mathematical objects into types.

## NEW LESSONS
### calculus
- Advanced Integration Techniques — Explore special functions and integral transforms.
- Calculus of Variations — Understand how to find functions that optimize quantities.
- Differential Forms — Study exterior calculus and its applications in geometry and physics.

### linear-algebra
- Tensor Algebra — Explore tensors and their applications in physics and engineering.
- Sparse Matrices — Learn efficient storage and computation with sparse matrices.
- Matrix Decompositions — Discover advanced decompositions like QR and LU factorizations.

### topology
- Algebraic Topology — Introduce homology and cohomology groups.
- Manifolds — Study differentiable manifolds and their properties.
- Fiber Bundles — Explore the concept of fiber bundles in topology.

### number-theory
- Elliptic Curves — Study the arithmetic of elliptic curves and their applications.
- Analytic Methods — Use complex analysis to solve number-theoretic problems.
- Algebraic Number Theory — Explore number fields and their rings of integers.

### probability
- Stochastic Processes — Introduce Poisson processes, Brownian motion, and Markov chains.
- Bayesian Networks — Learn about graphical models for probabilistic relationships.
- Randomized Algorithms — Study algorithms that use randomness to solve problems.

### group-theory
- Representation Theory — Explore how groups can be represented by matrices.
- Group Cohomology — Study cohomology of groups and its applications.
- Infinite Groups — Explore properties of infinite groups and their subgroups.

### graph-theory
- Spectral Graph Theory — Use eigenvalues and eigenvectors to analyze graphs.
- Graph Coloring Algorithms — Develop efficient algorithms for graph coloring.
- Graph Isomorphism — Study methods to determine if two graphs are isomorphic.

### differential-geometry
- General Relativity — Explore the geometry of spacetime in general relativity.
- Symplectic Geometry — Study the geometry of phase spaces in classical mechanics.
- Geometric Flows — Explore partial differential equations that describe the evolution of geometric shapes.

### set-theory
- Forcing — Learn about forcing and its applications in set theory.
- Large Cardinals — Explore large cardinal axioms and their consistency.
- Set-Theoretic Topology — Study topological spaces in the context of set theory.

### fractals
- Fractal Dimensions — Understand the concept of fractal dimension and its applications.
- Iterated Function Systems — Explore fractals generated by iterated function systems.
- Fractals in Nature — Study natural phenomena that exhibit fractal properties.

### fourier-analysis
- Wavelet Analysis — Explore the theory and applications of wavelets.
- Time-Frequency Analysis — Study the time-frequency representation of signals.
- Sampling Theory — Explore the mathematical foundations of signal sampling.

### complex-analysis
- Riemann Zeta Function — Study the Riemann zeta function and its zeros.
- Complex Dynamics — Explore the dynamics of complex functions.
- Elliptic Functions — Study elliptic functions and their applications.

### computation
- Quantum Computing — Introduce the basics of quantum computing and algorithms.
- Formal Verification — Learn about methods for formally verifying software correctness.
- Computable Analysis — Study the theory of computable functions on real numbers.

### philosophy-of-math
- Constructivism — Explore the philosophy of mathematics from a constructive perspective.
- Structuralism — Study the structuralist approach to mathematics.
- Mathematical Pluralism — Explore the idea that there are multiple valid mathematical frameworks.

### combinatorics
- Ramsey Theory — Study the conditions under which order must appear.
- Probabilistic Combinatorics — Use probability to solve combinatorial problems.
- Algebraic Combinatorics — Explore the intersection of combinatorics and algebra.

### differential-equations
- Sturm-Liouville Theory — Study eigenvalue problems for differential equations.
- Boundary Value Problems — Explore methods for solving boundary value problems.
- Delay Differential Equations — Introduce and analyze equations with delays.
