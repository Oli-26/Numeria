## Systemic notes

- The short 25-question topics (memetics, conspiracy, simulation, numerology, lost cities, ouparts, cold-war psi, vanishings) were mostly solid; errors clustered in dates and popular anecdotes (Mary Celeste logbook and date, UVB-76 start date, Maya population, Angkor "largest city").
- Number stations and Cicada 3301 had invented-looking specifics (Russian "information embargo" lift, HM01 in the Cuban Five and Myers cases, ENIGMA codes, "no finisher ever spoke"). Treat remaining unsourced specifics in those two topics with suspicion.
- The three long advanced topics (cult dynamics, hyperstition, occult history) are dense with names, books and years; most checked out, but misattributions recur (Wilding/Bruno, Sleeper Agents, Mathers's 777, Partridge "coined", Crowley "led" K2). Hyperstition's reliance on paraphrased CCRU/Land material is loose in places.
- FindTheError items in cult-dynamics and hyperstition frequently had the keyed step off by one or a second false step; six fixed, one flagged. Worth a systematic pass on FindTheError items in other fields too.
