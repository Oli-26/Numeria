## Flagged (not edited)

- philosophy-logic / phil-log-02-c3: circularity test "ask 'what would convince me this is wrong?'". This describes unfalsifiability rather than circular reasoning. It is hedged with "may", so I left it.
- philosophy-epistemology / phil-epi-03-c3 + q13: no-false-lemmas is attributed to Harman. It is usually credited first to Michael Clark (1963), with Harman's version in 1973.
- philosophy-epistemology / phil-epi-q15: the stopped-clock case is said to have "no false lemma". This is contested: many argue the belief that the clock works is an implicit false lemma.
- philosophy-ethics / phil-eth-04-c3: care ethics is described as "extending virtue ethics". Many care ethicists treat it as a distinct theory.
- philosophy-metaphysics / phil-meta-q25: "Lewis's counterfactual theory ... remains the dominant framework" is contestable, since interventionist accounts (Woodward) are equally prominent.
- philosophy-mind / phil-mind-03-c3 + q14: Dennett is labelled an eliminativist. He is often classed differently, and the Churchlands are the paradigm eliminativists.
- philosophy-german-idealism / phil-gi-02-c3: "Kant calls this practical freedom". Noumenal agency is what Kant calls transcendental freedom, and the terms are muddled here.
- philosophy-eastern / phil-ea-05-c2: "see your nature and become Buddha" is called the Linji (Rinzai) slogan. It is the general Chán formula traditionally attributed to Bodhidharma.
- philosophy-postmodernism / pomo-03-c2: the parenthetical examples for Baudrillard's four phases (for example the "Renaissance icon") are the author's own glosses, not Baudrillard's.
- philosophy-postmodernism / pomo-05-c1: "What we call 'truth' is what our peers ... let us get away with saying". In Rorty this is chiefly a claim about justification.
- philosophy-postmodernism / pomo-06-c1: Jencks's Pruitt–Igoe date of 15 July 1972, 3:32 p.m. The first building was demolished on 16 March 1972. The lesson presents the date as Jencks's joke, so it is acceptable.
