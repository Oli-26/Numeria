## Systemic notes

- The two advanced topics (geo-geochem, geo-astrobiology) are technically excellent: decay constants, calibrations, dates and attributions almost all check out. Their errors were isolated (222Rn half-life, an HSE abundance off by 10x, an oxygen/sulfur MIF mix-up).
- The introductory geology topics carry the familiar teaching-lore errors: superlatives attached to the wrong object (Mauna Loa vs Mauna Kea, Sognefjord), 'overdue' magnetic reversals, Yellowstone framed as periodic, and an energy-ratio slip for earthquake magnitudes.
- A figure caption (Greenland ice cores 'back 800,000 years') was wrong where the body text was right, so captions are worth checking separately from concept text.
- geology-tectonics needed the most fixes per concept; geology-metals and geology-paleontology were the cleanest of the introductory set.
