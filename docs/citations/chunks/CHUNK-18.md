## Rejected (3)

- `hist-med-01-c3` "codified Roman law into the Corpus Juris Civilis": reviewer: The page title is only 'The Corpus Juris Civilis', without the proposed subtitle. The abstract says it was 'created by order of Byzantine Emperor Justinian I to compile the laws in force at the time' but never mentions Roman law or codification. The quote is also not verbatim ('created by order...'). The source does not support the claim that Justinian codified Roman law. (https://scholarship.law.wm.edu/libpubs/118/)
- `hist-mod-01-c2` "Between 1980 and 2020, China's GDP grew at roughly 9% per year, which is unprece": reviewer: World Bank says growth averaged over 9% since 1978, not 1980-2020, and the page never says 'unprecedented'; claim is broader and different in period. (https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/china/overview)
- `hist-rel-01-c3` "Hinduism has ~1.2 billion adherents, mostly in India and Nepal.": reviewer: The Pew page is real and the quote is on it. It supports 'nearly 1.2 billion'. It says India holds 95% of Hindus, and it names Nepal only as a country where Hindus are a majority. It does not say Hindus live mostly in India and Nepal, so the claim goes beyond the source. (https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/hindu-population-change/)
