## Contradicted by a source (3)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `rrel-01-c1`: "The older "two-tier" model, which he traced back to David Hume"  
  Source says: Not checked. ()
- `rrel-01-c1`: "In 386 Bishop Ambrose of Milan announced the discovery of the bodies of two martyrs, Gervasius and Protasius, had them carried in procession, and buried them beneath the altar of his new basilica."  
  Source says: Augustine's Confessions 9.7 describes the bodies being 'dug up and with due honour transferred to the Ambrosian Basilica'. It does not say they were buried beneath the altar, which comes from Ambrose's letters and was not checked; Augustine also calls it a vision, not an announcement. Mild mismatch only. (https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/110109.htm)
- `rrel-01-c3`: "After the Buddha's cremation at Kusinara, A Brahmin named Dona ... a latecomer clan received the embers"  
  Source says: The Vajira and Story translation says the Moriyas were given 'the ashes', not embers (other translations render it as embers or charcoal). Minor translation variance, not a real contradiction. (https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/dn/dn.16.1-6.vaji.html)
