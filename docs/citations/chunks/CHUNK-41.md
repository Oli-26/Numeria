The fetched content does not mention cosmic rays, the upper atmosphere or sea level, so the claim is broader than the source supports. (https://hyperphysics.gsu.edu/hbase/Relativ/muon.html)
- `phy-sol-01-c1` "Modern photovoltaics began at Bell Labs in 1954, when Chapin, Fuller, and Pearso": reviewer: Page supports Bell Labs 1954 and 6% efficiency, but never names Chapin, Fuller or Pearson or calls it the first practical cell, so the claim is broader than the source.; quote not found on page (https://www1.eere.energy.gov/analysis/pdfs/solar_pv.pdf)
- `phy-sol-01-c1` "Modern photovoltaics began at Bell Labs in 1954, when Chapin, Fuller, and Pearso": reviewer: Names, Bell Labs and 1954 match, but the same entry gives 4% efficiency (later 11%), contradicting the claim's ~6%. (https://www1.eere.energy.gov/solar/pdfs/solar_timeline.pdf)
- `phys-thermo-01-c3` "The Sun heats Earth this way across 150 million kilometres of vacuum.": reviewer: NASA page supports only the 150 million km Earth-Sun distance.
