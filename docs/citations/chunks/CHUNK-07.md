## Contradicted by a source (2)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `econ-demo-01-c3`: "Bloom, Williamson and others estimate ~25-40% of East Asian GDP growth 1965-95 came from demographic factors."  
  Source says: NBER Digest on Bloom and Williamson (1998): 'one-third to one-half of the growth experienced in East Asia between 1965 and 1990 could be attributable to the region's ability to take advantage of this demographic gift.' So the range is about 33-50% and the period is 1965-90, not 25-40% and 1965-95. (https://www.nber.org/digest/jul98/demographic-transitions-and-economic-miracles-emerging-asia)
- `econ-env-01-c4`: "comparisons with the SO₂ trading system (1995-2005) showed cost savings of 30-50% relative to command-and-control alternatives."  
  Source says: Chan et al. estimate ex post cost savings of $210 million to $240 million (US$1995) per year, which they call a much smaller estimate than Carlson et al. (2000) and a fraction of what the EPA forecast in 1992. This does not confirm 30-50%, and the lesson should cite a specific study or soften the figure. (https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w21383/w21383.pdf)
