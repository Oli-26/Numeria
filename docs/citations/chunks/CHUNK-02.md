## Contradicted by a source (6)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `synb-01-c3`: "The International Genetically Engineered Machine competition (iGEM) was launched at MIT in 2004 as a summer course"  
  Source says: iGEM began with a 2003 MIT course, and in 2004 it started as a competition with student teams from five schools. So it began as a course in 2003 (IAP), and 2004 was the first competition. Note the Technology Review page says the Edinburgh colour readout was red/yellow, which is consistent with the lesson's 'color-changing' wording. (https://blog.igem.org/blog/2023/6/28/celebrating-20-years-of-standard-biological-parts)
- `mgen-01-c2`: "cryo-EM tomography of intact nuclei (Ou et al., 2017)"  
  Source says: Ou et al. used ChromEMT, a DNA-labelling dye method (DAB polymerisation) with electron microscopy tomography, not cryo-EM. The abstract says chromatin is a disordered 5- to 24-nanometer-diameter curvilinear chain. It does not itself say the 30 nm fibre is absent. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28751582/)
- `mgen-01-c1`: "A nucleosome is 147 base pairs of DNA ... The crystal structure (Luger et al., 1997)"  
  Source says: The Luger et al. 1997 abstract refers to 146 base pairs of DNA. The 147 bp figure comes from the later Davey et al. 2002 structure. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9305837/)
- `mgen-01-c2`: "Acute auxin-induced degradation of cohesin (Rao et al., 2017) abolishes TADs within hours"  
  Source says: The abstract says all loop domains are eliminated. It does not say 'TADs' or 'within hours'; the paper's wording is loop domains. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28985562/)
- `bio-mic-01-c3`: "Mycorrhizal fungi associate with ~90% of plant roots, exchanging nutrients for sugars."  
  Source says: Field & Pressel (2018), New Phytologist 220:996-1011, doi 10.1111/nph.15158 (Tansley review): the symbiosis occurs between 'an estimated 85% of land plants' and Mucoromycota fungi, and arbuscular mycorrhiza alone covers c. 74% of plant species. The lesson says ~90% and 'plant roots' rather than plant species; suggest 'roughly 85% of land plant species'. (https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/131022/8/Field_et_al-2018-New_Phytologist.pdf)
- `bio-mar-01-c1`: "Deeper still are the bathyal (200-2000 m), abyssal (2000-6000 m) and hadal (>6000 m) zones."  
  Source says: WHOI gives the abyss as 3,000 to 6,500 m and the hadal zone as 6,000 to 11,000 m; boundaries vary by convention, so the lesson's 2000 m abyssal start differs from WHOI. (https://www.whoi.edu/know-your-ocean/ocean-topics/how-the-ocean-works/ocean-zones/)
