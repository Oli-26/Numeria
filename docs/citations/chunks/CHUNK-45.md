## Contradicted by a source (2)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `ramx-01-c1`: "Guesses ran as high as 20,000"  
  Source says: NEH (Kentucky Humanities) says around 20,000 people attended, with estimates ranging from 10,000 to 30,000, so 20,000 is a typical figure, not the highest guess. Suggest 'estimates ran as high as 30,000'. (https://www.neh.gov/article/cane-ridge)
- `psych-soc-01-c2`: "Milgram predicted only 1–3% would go to the maximum."  
  Source says: Milgram's 1963 paper reports that respondents he polled (a class) predicted an insignificant minority would go to the end: estimates ranged from 0 to 3%, with a class mean of 1.2%. So it was a survey of others, with a 0–3% range, not Milgram's own 1–3% prediction. (https://www.columbia.edu/cu/psychology/terrace/w1001/readings/milgram.pdf)
