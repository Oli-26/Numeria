## Contradicted by a source (1)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `grp-01-c3`: "Matrix multiplication is non-commutative too, and matrices form a group (GLₙ)."  
  Source says: The general linear group GL_n(R) over a ring R with identity is the group of all invertible n×n matrices with entries in R. So only invertible matrices form a group; matrices in general do not (singular matrices have no inverse). Lesson should say invertible matrices. (https://mathworld.wolfram.com/GeneralLinearGroup.html)
