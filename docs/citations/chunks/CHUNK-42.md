## Contradicted by a source (3)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `pol-geo-01-c2`: "The architects of US containment in the late 1940s (Kennan, Acheson, the early planners of NATO and the Marshall Plan) operated within a framework recognisably descended from his."  
  Source says: Peer-reviewed article states: "Although the founder of the containment policy George F. Kennan made no references to heartland theory or Spykman". The claim of descent from Mackinder is at least contested and should be hedged. (https://gh.copernicus.org/articles/78/183/2023/)
- `pol-cmp-01-c2`: "The political scientist Steven Levitsky has called the broader pattern competitive authoritarianism"  
  Source says: The term comes from Steven Levitsky and Lucan A. Way, 'The Rise of Competitive Authoritarianism', Journal of Democracy, April 2002. Attribution to Levitsky alone is incomplete; it should credit Levitsky and Way. (https://ned.org/journal-of-democracy-classics-the-rise-of-competitive-authoritarianism-levitsky-way)
- `pol-cmp-01-c1`: "Robert Dahl's influential expansion (Polyarchy, 1971) added the operational conditions Schumpeter took for granted"  
  Source says: The opened source is Dahl's On Democracy (1998), which lists six institutions including inclusive citizenship. The lesson's exact list matches this later work, not verified against Polyarchy (1971), which gave a different formulation of conditions. (https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.tufts.edu/dist/a/4100/files/2019/07/Dahl-Ch8.pdf)
