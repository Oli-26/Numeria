## Contradicted by a source (2)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `misc-van-01-c1`: "around 117 English colonists led by John White established a settlement on Roanoke Island"  
  Source says: NPS: 'The 1587 voyage to Roanoke, consisting of 118 men, women, and children'; NCpedia says approximately 150. Counts vary by source, so 'around 117' is defensible but not uniform. (https://www.nps.gov/fora/learn/historyculture/1587-the-lost-colony.htm)
- `misc-und-01-c2`: "The average inscription is about 5 signs and the longest about 26, so statistical signatures barely emerge."  
  Source says: Farmer, Sproat and Witzel, 'The Collapse of the Indus-Script Thesis' (Electronic Journal of Vedic Studies 11(2), 2004): 'The longest on one surface has 17 symbols... the average length of the 2,905 objects carrying Indus symbols catalogued in Mahadevan's standard concordance is 4.6 signs long.' The average of about 5 is roughly consistent, but the longest is 17 signs on a single surface, not 26 (26 may count signs across several faces, which I could not verify). The source is a published journal paper but I found no DOI. (https://www.safarmer.com/fsw2.pdf)
