## Contradicted by a source (2)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `phys-cm-01-c3`: "Pure silicon is an insulator, but doped silicon, with one phosphorus per million atoms, is a semiconductor"  
  Source says: MIT OCW treats intrinsic (pure) silicon as a semiconductor with conductivity of about 0.0002 inverse ohm cm; doping at about one phosphorus per million silicon atoms makes it about 3,000 times more conductive. Pure silicon is not an insulator; doping raises conductivity within a semiconductor. (https://ocw.tau.edu.ng/courses/mechanical-engineering/2-627-fundamentals-of-photovoltaics-fall-2013/tutorial-videos/tutorial-doping)
- `phys-bio-01-c3`: "Stop the flagellum and the bacterium stops within a few percent of its body length."  
  Source says: Datta (2024, arXiv:2410.20648, inspired by Purcell) estimates an E. coli coasting distance of about 0.1 Angstrom, a tenth the size of a hydrogen atom, i.e. about 10^-4 of body length, far below a few percent. Quote: "The viscous drag of the surrounding fluid plays such a dominant role for microbes that it stops them from coasting essentially instantaneously." Suggest rewording to say it stops within a fraction of an atomic diameter. (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.20648)
