## Contradicted by a source (5)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `canc-01-c3`: "added four more capabilities, bringing the total to 14. The new entries are unlocking phenotypic plasticity, nonmutational epigenetic reprogramming, polymorphic microbiomes, and senescent cells."  
  Source says: The 2022 abstract proposes only phenotypic plasticity as a possible distinct hallmark capability. Nonmutational epigenetic reprogramming and polymorphic microbiomes are called enabling characteristics, and senescent cells are proposed as an added tumor-microenvironment cell type. (Abstract seen only as a tool paraphrase; the full text was blocked with 403.) (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35022204/)
- `bio-dev-01-c3`: "Lewis Wolpert put it this way: "It is not birth, marriage, or death, but gastrulation, which is truly the most important time in your life.""  
  Source says: Minor: Hopwood gives the wording without commas ('birth, marriage or death, but gastrulation which is'). Wolpert said it in 1979, first printed by Jonathan Slack in From Egg to Embryo (1983). (https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/277AA3AE743772CB4335A98836564E48/core-reader)
- `bio-imm-01-c2`: "Together the repertoire covers ~10¹¹ possible specificities, enough to recognize essentially any antigen."  
  Source says: Attaf, Huseby & Sewell (2015, Cell Mol Immunol, doi:10.1038/cmi.2014.134): 'The theoretical diversity in the TCR-αβ repertoire is estimated at 10^15 clones in mice and 10^18 in humans.' The realized peripheral repertoire is far smaller (millions of clonotypes). No 10^11 figure appears; the lesson number should be corrected or reworded (e.g. theoretical diversity far exceeds 10^11 for TCRs, realized repertoire is ~10^7). (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC4496535/fullTextXML)
- `bio-bot-01-c1`: "about 12,000 ferns and 20,000 bryophytes"  
  Source says: Christenhusz and Byng 2016 give ferns 10,560 (plus lycophytes separately), gymnosperms 1,079, liverworts ca 9,000, hornworts ca 225, mosses 12,700 (bryophytes about 21,900). Angiosperms 295,383 matches 'about 300,000' (Kew 2016 later reports about 369,000). Consider 'about 10,000 ferns' and 'about 22,000 bryophytes'. (https://phytotaxa.mapress.com/pt/article/view/phytotaxa.261.3.1)
- `bio-bot-01-c3`: "About 140 million years ago, flowering plants diversified rapidly"  
  Source says: Source only says flowering plants exploded in diversity in the Early Cretaceous (roughly 145-100 Ma); the fossil record shows the great radiation in the mid-Cretaceous around 100 Ma. 'About 140 Ma' is the early edge; 'during the Cretaceous' is safer. (https://www.botanicgardens.org.au/discover-and-learn/watch-listen-read/when-did-flowering-plants-appear-earth)
