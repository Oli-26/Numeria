## Contradicted by a source (3)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `hist-rus-01-c3`: "It began as a wooden fort founded in 1147"  
  Source says: University of Washington chronology: 1147 is the 'First mention of Moscow in the chronicles. It is a minor, border outpost of the Grand Prince of Vladimir.' 1147 is the first mention, not a founding date or a wooden fort. (http://faculty.washington.edu/dwaugh/hstam443/m-chron.html)
- `hist-rus-01-c3`: "Ivan III also took the title Tsar (a contraction of Caesar)"  
  Source says: Goldfrank in the Encyclopedia of Russian History: 'in 1547 Ivan IV was crowned officially as tsar as well as grand prince.' The official tsar title dates to Ivan IV; Ivan III used it only informally. (https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/modern-europe/russian-soviet-and-cis-history/muscovy)
- `hist-rus-01-c3`: "wouldn't pay tribute in 1480 (the 'Great Stand on the Ugra River') and effectively ended Mongol overlordship"  
  Source says: Krom: the end of the Mongol yoke at the Ugra is Russian historical tradition. Gorskii argues 'the liberation should be dated not to 1480 but to 1472', and Vernadsky argues for the 1450s. The date is contested. (https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/ugra-river-battle)
