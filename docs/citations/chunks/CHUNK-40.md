## Contradicted by a source (2)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `phys-part-01-c2`: "It's a property of QCD that has resisted analytical proof, and proving it is one of the Millennium Prize Problems."  
  Source says: The Clay Millennium problem is the Yang-Mills existence and mass gap problem. The page does not mention quark confinement; it says no proof of the mass gap property is known. Confinement is related but not itself the stated prize problem, so the lesson should say the mass gap problem instead, or soften the wording. (https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/)
- `phys-nuc-01-c2`: "curve that peaks near iron-56"  
  Source says: Fe-56 is the third most tightly bound nuclide, exceeded only by 58Fe and 62Ni; 62Ni is the most tightly bound. So the peak is at nickel-62, with iron-56 close (8.8 MeV/nucleon). Lesson could say 'near iron-56 and nickel-62'. (https://hyperphysics.gsu.edu/hbase/NucEne/nucbin.html)
