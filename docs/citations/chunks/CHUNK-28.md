(https://www.nist.gov/si-redefinition/kelvin-present-realization)
- `mat-str-01-c2` "BCC α-Fe is ferromagnetic (up to its Curie point, 770 °C)": reviewer: The page is a deformation-mechanism chapter titled 'Pure Iron and Ferrous Alloys', with no named authors, so the proposed title and attribution do not match. It only mentions loss of ferromagnetism at 770°C in passing. It never says BCC alpha-Fe is ferromagnetic, so the claim is not supported exactly. (https://defmech.engineering.dartmouth.edu/chapter_8.htm)
- `topm-01-c1` "It has an insulating bulk gap (~0.1-0.3 eV in Bi₂Se₃), a single Dirac cone on ea": reviewer: Claim says gap ~0.1-0.3 eV; the paper gives about 0.3 eV (0.35 eV measured) and never mentions 0.1 eV. The claim also bundles the ARPES Dirac cone, which the quote does not cover. (https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/0908.3513)
