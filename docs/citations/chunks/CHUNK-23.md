## Contradicted by a source (2)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `ling-hist-01-c1`: "About 400 language families are known."  
  Source says: Glottolog: "Languages are classified (see below) into 246 families and 183 isolates, i.e., one-member families." So about 246 multi-member families (about 429 counting isolates). Ethnologue (search snippet only, page 403) gives 142 families. The figure depends on the database and on whether isolates count; the lesson should say so. (https://glottolog.org/glottolog/glottologinformation)
- `ling-hist-01-c1`: "Proto-Indo-European (PIE), spoken roughly 5,000–7,000 years ago"  
  Source says: Mild caveat only: Penn says PIE was spoken at least six thousand years ago; the opened USU page gives 5000-2000 BCE for the culture and Bucknell says around 5,000 BC. Estimates vary, and Anatolian-hypothesis work gives 8,000+ years, so the range is a conventional estimate. (https://plc.sas.upenn.edu/node/5401)
