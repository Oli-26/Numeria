## Contradicted by a source (2)

These are likely errors in the lessons.

- `hist-exp-01-c2`: "The Marquesas, Society Islands, and Hawai'i by ~700–1000 CE."  
  Source says: Wilmshurst et al. 2011: East Polynesian colonization was earliest in the Society Islands at A.D. ~1025-1120, four centuries later than previously assumed, then one major pulse to all remaining islands A.D. ~1190-1290. So the 700-1000 CE dates are too early. (https://researchportalplus.anu.edu.au/en/publications/high-precision-radiocarbon-dating-shows-recent-and-rapid-initial-/)
- `hist-em-01-c1`: "When Constantinople fell in 1453, Greek scholars carried manuscripts west."  
  Source says: Royal Holloway (J. Harris, 2019 talk): it is a perennial myth that scholars escaped with Greek classics under their arms. Many Italians learned Greek without Byzantine help, though exiles did affect Greek studies in Italy. The lesson overstates a sudden 1453 flight of scholars. (https://pure.royalholloway.ac.uk/en/activities/byzantium-and-the-renaissance)
