FULL DESCRIPTION
Same voters, different winners. Nous lets you play with the machinery of power: redraw a state's seven districts and watch the same 25 Blue and 24 Red voters elect 6–1 either way, then measure the wasted votes and the efficiency gap. Count one electorate four ways (plurality, instant runoff, approval and proportional) and watch the seats move. Pick the words for a front page and see how they slant the story.

STRANGE POLITICS
True oddities of power, every claim cited. Venice elected its doge in ten rounds of lottery and ballot for five centuries; try the machine yourself. Athens picked juries with a stone allotment machine and voted rivals into exile on pottery shards, and 190 shards naming Themistocles turned out to be written by just 14 hands. Elections have been decided by drawing a film canister from a bowl and won by candidates who had already died, and a 1982 term paper that got a C ended with a new amendment to the US Constitution.
