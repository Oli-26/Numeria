History is stranger than the textbooks let on. Nous is a learning app for the odd, overlooked and genuinely surprising side of history, religion, science and philosophy, written from mainstream scholarship and backed by thousands of cited sources.

Find out what "cargo cults" really were, and where the label came from. Why Holbein hid a stretched skull in The Ambassadors. Which Christianities lost out before there was an agreed Bible. Why the world kept not ending, and what believers did next.

NEW: THREE FREE SKILL COURSES
Real skills you can show off, each a story map of short lessons with a task to try after every one.

PICK A CARD: card magic and sleight of hand
Learn magic tricks with an ordinary deck of cards. Start with the key card and self-working math tricks (the 21-card trick, Gilbreath's shuffle, the Kruskal count, a five-card mind-reading code), then the sleights: shuffles, false shuffles, the jog control, the double lift and card forces. Learn misdirection from the psychology of attention, and meet the masters: Robert-Houdin, Erdnase, Dai Vernon fooling Houdini and the memorized deck.

MEMORY PALACE: memory techniques of the memory champions
The 2,500-year-old method of loci, step by step. Build a palace in your head, then memorize lists, numbers, names and faces, and finally a whole shuffled deck of cards. Real drills, and the science of why it works.

FROM SCRATCH: survival skills and bushcraft
Stranded in the wild, or after the lights go out for good: fire starting, water purification, shelter, first aid, knots, foraging and navigation, then rebuild with building, chemistry, metalwork and electricity. Over 180 diagrams.
