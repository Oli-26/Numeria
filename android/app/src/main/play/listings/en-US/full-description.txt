History is stranger than the textbooks let on. Nous is a learning app for the odd, overlooked and genuinely surprising side of history, religion, science and philosophy, written from mainstream scholarship and backed by thousands of cited sources.

Find out what "cargo cults" really were, and where the label came from. Why Holbein hid a stretched skull in The Ambassadors. Which Christianities lost out before there was an agreed Bible. Why the world kept not ending, and what believers did next.

NEW: FROM SCRATCH
A survival course with its own story map. Stranded in the wild, or after the lights go out for good, learn fire, water, shelter, first aid, knots, food and navigation, then rebuild: building, chemistry, metal and electricity. Over 180 diagrams, and a real task to try after every lesson.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 19 subjects and over 300 topics, from Sacred Forgeries and Strange Saints to Number Stations, Undeciphered Scripts and the History of Music
• Nearly 2,000 short lessons and almost 13,000 quiz questions, with explanations
• Art lessons with the paintings themselves: tap any picture to zoom in
• The big questions: what the universe is made of, how life began, what a mind is
• The core subjects too: physics, biology, chemistry, mathematics, economics, literature and more

HOW IT WORKS
• Lessons take a few minutes. Guess first, then learn why.
• Every answer is explained, and a review queue brings back what you missed
• Over 4,600 citations to papers, books and museums, so you can check the facts yourself
• Hands-on simulators: particle sandbox, molecule builder, evolution simulator, deep time scroller, era map
• A knowledge graph shows how every topic connects
• A daily pick, a daily challenge and a fortune cookie from the subject you're studying
• Streaks, XP and ranks, if you like that sort of thing

STRANGE, NOT MADE UP
Legends are labeled as legends, debates are shown as debates, and the evidence comes first.

No account needed, no ads, and lessons work offline.
