History is stranger than the textbooks let on, and so is everything else. Nous is a learning app for the odd, overlooked and genuinely surprising side of every subject, written from mainstream scholarship and backed by over 6,000 cited sources.

NEW: THE STRANGE SERIES
A Strange topic for every subject, with 60 lessons of true oddities. A natural nuclear reactor that ran itself in Gabon two billion years ago. A Greenland shark that may be 400 years old. The "illegal" prime number. A horse that seemed to do sums. A town where you can't be buried. A pitch drop that falls about once a decade, a jellyfish that runs its life backward, and the dictionary's ghost word.

Find out what "cargo cults" really were, and where the label came from. Why Holbein hid a stretched skull in The Ambassadors. Which Christianities lost out before there was an agreed Bible. Why the world kept not ending, and what believers did next.

22 HANDS-ON LABS
Redraw a state's districts and watch the same voters elect 6–1 either way. Elect a doge with Venice's ten rounds of lottery and ballot. Test a rock with steel and acid, sail the spice route, and zoom from the Planck length to the whole observable universe.

THREE FREE SKILL COURSES
Real skills you can show off, each a story map of short lessons with a task to try after every one.

PICK A CARD: card magic and sleight of hand
Start with the key card and self-working math tricks (the 21-card trick, Gilbreath's shuffle, the Kruskal count), then the sleights: false shuffles, the double lift and card forces. Learn misdirection from the psychology of attention, and meet the masters, from Robert-Houdin to Dai Vernon fooling Houdini.

MEMORY PALACE: memory techniques of the memory champions
The 2,500-year-old method of loci, step by step. Memorize lists, numbers, names and faces, and finally a whole shuffled deck of cards.

FROM SCRATCH: survival skills and bushcraft
Fire starting, water purification, shelter, first aid, knots, foraging and navigation, then rebuild with building, chemistry, metalwork and electricity. Over 180 diagrams.
