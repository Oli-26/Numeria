## NEW PHYSICS TOPICS
- physics-optics: Light and Vision — Explore the behavior and properties of light, including reflection, refraction, and diffraction.
- physics-particle: Particle Physics — Investigate the fundamental particles and forces that make up matter, including quarks, leptons, and the Higgs boson.
- physics-condensed-matter: Condensed Matter Physics — Study the physical properties of solids and liquids, including crystal structures, phase transitions, and superconductivity.
- physics-fluids: Fluid Dynamics — Analyze the behavior of fluids, including fluid flow, turbulence, and boundary layers.
- physics-nuclear: Nuclear Physics — Examine the structure and interactions of atomic nuclei, including nuclear fusion and fission.
- physics-biophysics: Biophysics — Apply physical principles to understand biological systems, including molecular biology, cellular processes, and biomechanics.

## NEW CHEMISTRY TOPICS
- chem-polymers: Polymer Chemistry — Explore the synthesis, properties, and applications of polymers, including plastics, fibers, and gels.
- chem-green: Green Chemistry — Focus on sustainable chemical processes and materials that minimize environmental impact.
- chem-nuclear: Nuclear Chemistry — Study the reactions and applications of radioactive isotopes and nuclear energy.
- chem-computational: Computational Chemistry — Use computer models and simulations to predict chemical reactions and properties.
- chem-surface: Surface Chemistry — Investigate the behavior and properties of materials at their interfaces, including catalysts and coatings.
- chem-pharma: Pharmaceutical Chemistry — Explore the synthesis, properties, and applications of drugs and pharmaceuticals.

## NEW BIOLOGY TOPICS
- bio-microbiology: Microbiology — Study the structure, function, and genetics of microorganisms, including bacteria, viruses, and fungi.
- bio-virology: Virology — Focus on the study of viruses, including their replication, evolution, and impact on human health.
- bio-immunology: Immunology — Investigate the immune system and its role in protecting the body from pathogens.
- bio-botany: Botany — Explore the structure, function, and classification of plants, including their evolution and ecological roles.
- bio-developmental: Developmental Biology — Study the processes that control the growth and development of organisms from fertilization to adulthood.
- bio-marine: Marine Biology — Focus on the study of marine ecosystems, including marine life, oceanography, and conservation.

## NEW GEOLOGY TOPICS
- geology-geomorphology: Geomorphology — Investigate the processes that shape the Earth's surface, including erosion, deposition, and tectonic activity.
- geology-geochemistry: Geochemistry — Explore the chemical composition and processes of rocks, soils, and fluids in the Earth system.
- geology-planetary: Planetary Geology — Study the geology of other planets and moons in our solar system.
- geology-paleoclimate: Paleoclimate — Focus on reconstructing past climates using geological evidence, including ice cores and fossil records.
- geology-geophysics: Geophysics — Investigate the physical properties and processes of the Earth, including seismology, geodesy, and magnetism.
- geology-economic: Economic Geology — Explore the resources and processes involved in the extraction and use of minerals and fossil fuels.
- geology-soils: Soil Science — Study the formation, composition, and properties of soils, including their role in ecosystems and agriculture.

## NEW LESSONS
### physics-mechanics
- "Friction and Motion" — Explore the role of friction in various types of motion and its impact on the design of vehicles and machinery.

- "Heat Transfer in Insulated Systems" — Study the principles of heat transfer and insulation in materials and systems, and their applications in building design and energy efficiency.
